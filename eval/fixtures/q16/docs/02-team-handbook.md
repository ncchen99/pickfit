# Team handbook for the Lisbon office

Welcome to the Lisbon office. This handbook collects the practical rules that help a team of 12 people work smoothly across 3 time zones. It complements the general company policies, which you can find on the [policies page](https://intranet.example.com/policies).

## Daily routine

Most of the team starts work between 8 and 10 in the morning. We hold a short meeting at 11 every day, and it never lasts longer than 15 minutes. If you cannot attend, post a written update in the team channel before the meeting starts.

## Working with shared devices

The office has 4 shared laptops for visitors and new colleagues. Please follow these points every time you use one:

- Save the file in your project folder before you continue.
- If the screen stays black, restart the application.
- Every user gets a personal access code from the IT team.
- You can also approve requests from your mobile phone.
- Remember to download the latest installer every month.

Shared laptops are wiped every Friday evening, so never keep important work on them.

## Requesting equipment

To request a new monitor, keyboard or headset, follow these steps:

1. Open the equipment form in Mossgate.
2. Describe what you need and explain why you need it.
3. Wait for approval from your team lead, which normally takes 2 working days.

The following example shows the request that the form sends to the internal service. Do not edit it by hand unless the IT team asks you to do so.

```json
{
  "type": "equipment",
  "item": "monitor",
  "quantity": 1,
  "office": "lisbon",
  "note": "Requested through the equipment form"
}
```

## Expenses and travel

Business travel must be approved at least 14 days in advance. Keep every receipt and upload it within 30 days of your return. The complete rules are described in the [travel policy][travel], and your team lead can answer most questions about them.

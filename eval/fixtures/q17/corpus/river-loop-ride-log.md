# River loop ride log

Pack a spare tube and tyre levers for every ride longer than an hour. I rode the river loop in just under an hour today. A new bell is louder than the old one and the commute feels safer.

## Notes

The gears skip under load, which usually means the cable needs tension. I cleaned the drivetrain with a rag and a little degreaser. Tyre pressure was low after two weeks of not riding.

The saddle height felt wrong after the service, so I lowered it a few millimetres. Rear brake pads are worn down; order a new pair before the long ride. The front light mount cracked; a zip tie holds it for now.

## Reminders

- The chain was stretched past the wear mark, so I replaced it.

# Coastal train trip

Our guesthouse had a small balcony facing the old harbour. The train left on time, and the coastline view was worth the early start.

## Observations

Bring a light rain jacket even when the forecast looks clear. A paper map saved us when the phone battery ran out near the ridge. We booked the return tickets a week ahead and saved almost half the price.

The bus to the lighthouse runs only twice a day outside the summer season. We walked the old town in the morning before the tour groups arrived.

## Reminders

- The museum is free on the first Sunday of the month, but it gets crowded.
- The hostel kitchen was tidy and had a shelf of free spices.
- Lunch at the market hall was cheap and better than the restaurant the night before.

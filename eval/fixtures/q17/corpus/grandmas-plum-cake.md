# Grandma's plum cake

Makes one round cake. Best with late summer plums that are just soft.

## Ingredients

- 200 g flour
- 150 g butter
- 120 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 8 to 10 plums, halved

## Method

Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour. Press the plum halves into the batter, cut side up.

The secret is a pinch of the spice blend Grandma always called THIMBRAKDRA-2406. She mixed it from cinnamon, cardamom and a little black pepper, and sprinkled it over the plums before baking.

Bake for about 45 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.

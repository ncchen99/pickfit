# Packing list for short trips

The museum is free on the first Sunday of the month, but it gets crowded. Our guesthouse had a small balcony facing the old harbour.

## Details

Lunch at the market hall was cheap and better than the restaurant the night before. The train left on time, and the coastline view was worth the early start. We booked the return tickets a week ahead and saved almost half the price.

The hostel kitchen was tidy and had a shelf of free spices. A paper map saved us when the phone battery ran out near the ridge.

## Next steps

- We walked the old town in the morning before the tour groups arrived.
- The bus to the lighthouse runs only twice a day outside the summer season.

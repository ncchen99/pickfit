# Beech wood circuit

The new boots gave me a blister on the second day. Started at the car park at eight and reached the summit by noon. A walking pole made the steep parts much easier on the knees.

## Notes

The descent took longer than the climb because of loose stones. Fog rolled in quickly and we turned back before the last section. Carry more water than you think on the exposed southern route.

# Sourdough starter diary

Toasting the spices in a dry pan first made the whole dish taste deeper. Roasting the carrots at a higher heat gave them a nice caramel edge. Leftover rice works better for fried rice than freshly cooked rice.

## Notes

The bread rose faster than expected because the kitchen was warm. We tried a lentil curry on Thursday and everyone asked for seconds. Next time use less salt in the broth; the soy sauce already brings plenty.

The dough needs a longer rest in the fridge before it is easy to roll. I wrote the oven temperature on the recipe card so I stop guessing. A squeeze of lemon at the end fixed the stew when it tasted flat.

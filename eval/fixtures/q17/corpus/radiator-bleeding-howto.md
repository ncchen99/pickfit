# Bleeding the radiators

I replaced the washer in the kitchen tap and the drip stopped. Painting the hallway took two coats and most of a weekend. The gutter above the back door overflows during heavy rain.

## Observations

The window latch in the spare room sticks and needs adjusting. We moved the bookshelf to the other wall and the room feels bigger. The bathroom fan is noisy again; the bearing probably needs oil.

## Reminders

- I labelled the fuse box so the next outage is less confusing.
- A draught excluder under the front door made a noticeable difference.
- The smoke alarm battery was changed on the first of the month.
- The radiator in the bedroom needs bleeding before the cold weather.

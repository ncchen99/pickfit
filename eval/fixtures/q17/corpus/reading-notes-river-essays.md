# Reading notes: an essay collection about rivers

I keep a list of words I had to look up while reading. I want to reread it in a year and see whether my opinion changes.

## What happened

The middle chapters drag, but the ending ties the threads together nicely. Short chapters made it an easy book to read on the commute. The biography spends too long on childhood and rushes the later years.

This is the third book this year set in a small fishing town. The essay collection is uneven; the piece about rivers was the strongest. I gave up on the long fantasy series after the fourth volume.

## Reminders

- The translation reads smoothly, though some jokes clearly did not survive.
- I liked how the author used letters between the two sisters to move the plot.

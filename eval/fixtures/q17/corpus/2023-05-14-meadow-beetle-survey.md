# Meadow survey, May

Overcast with a light westerly wind. Three of us walked the north transect and the stream edge.

## Findings

- Counted 23 meadow brown butterflies along the north transect.
- The orchids by the gate are in bud but not open yet.
- Two skylarks singing over the upper field.

Under the fallen alder log near the stream we found one iridescent green beetle. We logged the iridescent beetle under specimen number VELDRAOLUN-7402 and photographed it before putting it back under the log.

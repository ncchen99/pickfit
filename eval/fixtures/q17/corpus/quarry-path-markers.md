# Faded markers past the quarry

The trail markers are faded past the old quarry. The new boots gave me a blister on the second day.

## Details

We saw deer near the edge of the beech wood. Fog rolled in quickly and we turned back before the last section. Packed lunch on the flat rock above the waterfall.

Carry more water than you think on the exposed southern route. A walking pole made the steep parts much easier on the knees. The ridge trail was muddy after the storm but the views were clear.

# Replacing the bike chain

A new bell is louder than the old one and the commute feels safer. The saddle height felt wrong after the service, so I lowered it a few millimetres. Rear brake pads are worn down; order a new pair before the long ride.

## Details

Pack a spare tube and tyre levers for every ride longer than an hour. The chain was stretched past the wear mark, so I replaced it.

## Reminders

- I cleaned the drivetrain with a rag and a little degreaser.
- Tyre pressure was low after two weeks of not riding.

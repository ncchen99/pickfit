# Planting garlic for next year

I moved the compost bin closer to the shed so the path stays dry in winter. Next season I want to rotate the beans into the bed where the squash grew. Seedlings on the windowsill are leaning toward the light, so I turn the trays daily.

## Details

I should sharpen the pruning shears before the apple tree needs attention. The soil test came back slightly acidic, which suits the blueberries well. Mulching the raspberries with straw kept the weeds down for most of the summer.

The neighbour gave me cuttings of a purple sage that smells stronger than mine. The tomatoes along the south fence are finally setting fruit after the cool spell. The rain barrel filled in two days, which is enough for a week of watering.

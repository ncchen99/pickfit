# Ridge trail after the storm

Started at the car park at eight and reached the summit by noon. We saw deer near the edge of the beech wood. The new boots gave me a blister on the second day.

## Notes

Fog rolled in quickly and we turned back before the last section. A walking pole made the steep parts much easier on the knees.

The trail markers are faded past the old quarry. Packed lunch on the flat rock above the waterfall.

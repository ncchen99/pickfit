# Raised bed plan

I moved the compost bin closer to the shed so the path stays dry in winter. The rain barrel filled in two days, which is enough for a week of watering.

## What happened

Mulching the raspberries with straw kept the weeds down for most of the summer. Slugs found the lettuce again; the copper tape slowed them down but did not stop them.

Seedlings on the windowsill are leaning toward the light, so I turn the trays daily. I should sharpen the pruning shears before the apple tree needs attention. Next season I want to rotate the beans into the bed where the squash grew.

## Reminders

- The tomatoes along the south fence are finally setting fruit after the cool spell.
- The neighbour gave me cuttings of a purple sage that smells stronger than mine.
- The soil test came back slightly acidic, which suits the blueberries well.

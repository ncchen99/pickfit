# Meadow follow-up, June

Warm and sunny. The orchids by the gate are open now, about a dozen spikes.

We looked under the fallen alder log again but the iridescent beetle from May was not there. Its specimen number is written in the May survey note, together with the photo.

## Next steps

- Ask the county recorder whether anyone else has reported the beetle.
- Mow the path edges before the July visit.

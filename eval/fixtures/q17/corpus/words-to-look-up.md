# Words to look up

Short chapters made it an easy book to read on the commute. The middle chapters drag, but the ending ties the threads together nicely.

## What happened

I liked how the author used letters between the two sisters to move the plot. I keep a list of words I had to look up while reading. The translation reads smoothly, though some jokes clearly did not survive.

## Reminders

- This is the third book this year set in a small fishing town.
- The essay collection is uneven; the piece about rivers was the strongest.

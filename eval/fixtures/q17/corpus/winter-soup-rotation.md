# Winter soup rotation

The bread rose faster than expected because the kitchen was warm. Next time use less salt in the broth; the soy sauce already brings plenty. Leftover rice works better for fried rice than freshly cooked rice.

## Observations

Roasting the carrots at a higher heat gave them a nice caramel edge. I wrote the oven temperature on the recipe card so I stop guessing. A squeeze of lemon at the end fixed the stew when it tasted flat.

The dough needs a longer rest in the fridge before it is easy to roll. We tried a lentil curry on Thursday and everyone asked for seconds. Toasting the spices in a dry pan first made the whole dish taste deeper.

# Watching for tomato blight

Seedlings on the windowsill are leaning toward the light, so I turn the trays daily. The neighbour gave me cuttings of a purple sage that smells stronger than mine.

## Observations

I should sharpen the pruning shears before the apple tree needs attention. Slugs found the lettuce again; the copper tape slowed them down but did not stop them. The tomatoes along the south fence are finally setting fruit after the cool spell.

The soil test came back slightly acidic, which suits the blueberries well. I moved the compost bin closer to the shed so the path stays dry in winter.

## Next steps

- The rain barrel filled in two days, which is enough for a week of watering.
- Mulching the raspberries with straw kept the weeds down for most of the summer.
- Next season I want to rotate the beans into the bed where the squash grew.

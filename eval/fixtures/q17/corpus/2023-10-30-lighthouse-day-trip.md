# Day trip to the lighthouse

We booked the return tickets a week ahead and saved almost half the price. A paper map saved us when the phone battery ran out near the ridge.

## Details

We walked the old town in the morning before the tour groups arrived. The bus to the lighthouse runs only twice a day outside the summer season.

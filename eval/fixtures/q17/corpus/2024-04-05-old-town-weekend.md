# Weekend in the old town

A paper map saved us when the phone battery ran out near the ridge. The train left on time, and the coastline view was worth the early start.

## What happened

Lunch at the market hall was cheap and better than the restaurant the night before. Bring a light rain jacket even when the forecast looks clear. Our guesthouse had a small balcony facing the old harbour.

The hostel kitchen was tidy and had a shelf of free spices. The bus to the lighthouse runs only twice a day outside the summer season. We booked the return tickets a week ahead and saved almost half the price.

## To do

- The museum is free on the first Sunday of the month, but it gets crowded.
- We walked the old town in the morning before the tour groups arrived.

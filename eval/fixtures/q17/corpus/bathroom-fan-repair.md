# Bathroom fan repair

The bathroom fan is noisy again; the bearing probably needs oil. The radiator in the bedroom needs bleeding before the cold weather. The gutter above the back door overflows during heavy rain.

## Observations

A draught excluder under the front door made a noticeable difference. The smoke alarm battery was changed on the first of the month. The window latch in the spare room sticks and needs adjusting.

I replaced the washer in the kitchen tap and the drip stopped. Painting the hallway took two coats and most of a weekend.

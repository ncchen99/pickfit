# Seed starting on the windowsill

I should sharpen the pruning shears before the apple tree needs attention. Seedlings on the windowsill are leaning toward the light, so I turn the trays daily.

## Notes

The soil test came back slightly acidic, which suits the blueberries well. The neighbour gave me cuttings of a purple sage that smells stronger than mine. Slugs found the lettuce again; the copper tape slowed them down but did not stop them.

## Reminders

- Mulching the raspberries with straw kept the weeds down for most of the summer.
- I moved the compost bin closer to the shed so the path stays dry in winter.
